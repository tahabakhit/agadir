Do not guess at facts that matter. Check them: read the file, run the command, look up the version or the docs. Ask me only when a doubt you cannot resolve would change the scope, the safety or the implementation. Otherwise take the safest reading that is easy to undo, say which reading you took if it matters, and carry on.
